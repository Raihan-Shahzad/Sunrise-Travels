---
name: Appointment calendar sync
description: Calendar destination and privacy behavior for Sunrise Travels appointment bookings.
---

New appointment bookings belong in a dedicated Google Calendar named "Sunrise Travels Appointments", configured for Europe/London. Do not write customer appointments into the connected account's personal calendar or another unrelated calendar.

**Why:** The user chose a separate Sunrise Travels calendar rather than their primary personal calendar or a non-business calendar.

**How to apply:** Keep app-created events private, avoid inviting customers unless explicitly requested, and preserve London local appointment times.
